EAS Automation

EAS Automation is a private tool, built and operated by a single person, that helps organize documents received by email for construction-inspection projects. It is not a public service and has no public users.

What it does

  • Lets the operator search and read messages in the Gmail mailboxes the operator is authorized to use.
  • Saves attachments (plans, submittals, certifications, reports) into folders the operator chooses.
  • Creates drafts and applies labels when the operator asks. It does not send email on its own.

Your data

The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is accessed, how it is used, and how access can be revoked.
